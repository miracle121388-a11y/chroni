# Chroni community code of conduct

## Our standard

Chroni contributors, maintainers, testers, and users should make participation respectful, safe, and accessible. Constructive behavior includes welcoming different experience levels, discussing ideas and code rather than personal traits, accepting correction, giving actionable feedback, respecting privacy and consent, and taking responsibility for harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized language or attention, threats, doxxing, publishing private schedules/files/credentials, sustained disruption, impersonation, or retaliating against good-faith reports.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project events, and public spaces where someone represents Chroni. Security reports and participant research data require extra confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel. Do not include unnecessary personal data. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation depending on impact and pattern. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and handled by another maintainer where possible.

Good-faith reports will be reviewed without retaliation. Knowingly false reports or abuse of the reporting process may also be addressed. This policy does not replace platform terms or applicable law.
